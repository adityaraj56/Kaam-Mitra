The Problem

Millions of workers depend on experience that is difficult to prove outside their current workplace.

📄

No Portable Work History

Workers often do not have a simple digital record of their previous jobs and experience.

🔍

Difficult Verification

Employers may struggle to verify a worker's previous experience quickly.

💼

Missed Opportunities

Without trusted records, genuine experience can be harder to communicate to new employers.

How Kaam Mitra Works

A simple work record system from job completion to verified digital history.

1️⃣

Work Is Confirmed

An employer confirms the worker's role, duration and completed work.

2️⃣

Record Is Created

The information becomes part of the worker's digital work passbook.

3️⃣

Worker Shares It

Workers can share their work history with future employers.
